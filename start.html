Bounce

your mix, somewhere
other than the studio

Load a bounce, listen to it in the car or on the walk, and tap whenever something comes to you. It's about what you hear once you're out of the room. The note is pinned to where you are in the track, with the bar number.

When you're back in the project, share the whole list to yourself, timestamps and all, and work down it.

There's a gain stage into a limiter, so you can push a quiet premaster up to something you can judge on the move, and three bands of EQ if you want your track a little crispier, or the bottom end up or down. Both are monitoring only. Nothing is written to your file, and nothing is printed into the notes you share.

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone. If you tapped through from a message, hit the ⋯ or compass icon and choose Open in Safari first. The next step doesn't exist anywhere else.
  2. Tap the share button, scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from your home screen and load a bounce.
